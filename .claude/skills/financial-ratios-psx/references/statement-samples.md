## Common Pitfalls

1. **Revenue recognition**: Some companies use `Sales` instead of `Revenue from contracts with customers`
2. **Taxation line**: Sometimes split into `Current tax` + `Deferred tax`
3. **Borrowings**: `Current borrowings` may appear as `Short-term borrowings` or `Current portion of long-term debt`
4. **Equity**: Always verify if `Non-controlling interest` is shown separately — ROE uses only parent equity
5. **Shares outstanding**: May be in `Notes to accounts` section, not main statement
6. **Units**: Some companies report in PKR thousands, not millions — check the statement header
